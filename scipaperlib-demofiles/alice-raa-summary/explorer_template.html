About this compilation

“Most central” means the lowest available percentile interval in each selected pT table: commonly 0–5% or 0–10% in Pb–Pb, and 0–5%, 0–20% or 2–10% in p–Pb. The oxygen curves and the RpPb references are minimum-bias / NSD measurements. NSD means non-single-diffractive. Charge-conjugate states follow each source definition even when the legend uses one charge.

p–Pb event-activity selection and nuclear scaling require special care: see ALICE centrality-method paper. Original HEPData error labels are retained. Where only separate point-dependent components are supplied, their quadrature is used for display; this does not establish a covariance model. For centre-only tables, the horizontal width of a systematic box is decorative. Global terms mentioned only in paper text or table descriptions have not been reconstructed.
